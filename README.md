#  DiscoverMaroc

**DiscoverMaroc** is a personal web project built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, created out of a love for Morocco and a desire to showcase its richness to the world.

The idea behind this project is simple: Morocco is one of the most diverse and captivating countries in the world, yet it's often reduced to a handful of clichés. DiscoverMaroc aims to go beyond that — offering a curated, immersive experience that highlights the true depth of Moroccan culture, landscapes, and hospitality.

## What it offers

The platform lets users explore a wide range of Moroccan destinations — from the vibrant medinas of Marrakech and Fès, to the blue streets of Chefchaouen, the Atlantic breeze of Essaouira, and the golden silence of the Merzouga dunes. Each destination page features rich visuals, editorial descriptions, and practical travel information.

On the accommodation side, users can browse hotel listings and filter them by star rating to find options that match their budget and comfort level. A built-in review system lets travelers share their experiences, adding a community layer to the platform.

## Design philosophy

The UI draws inspiration from high-end travel magazines. The typography pairing of **Cormorant Garamond** (for headings) and **Jost** (for body text) creates an elegant yet readable aesthetic. The layout follows a chapter-based structure, and the color palette reflects Morocco itself — warm terracottas, desert golds, and deep blues.

## Technical approach

The entire project runs in the browser with no backend, no framework, and no build tools. Data is served from static JSON files, and all interactivity — carousels, filters, search, reviews — is handled with vanilla JavaScript. This was a deliberate choice to focus on mastering the fundamentals of web development.
